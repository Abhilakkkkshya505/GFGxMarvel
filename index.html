What is the Multiverse Event?

The barriers between dimensions have weakened. Bennett University's GFG Student Chapter is opening a rift — a two-day convergence where coders, builders, and thinkers from across disciplines collide in a single arena.

This isn't a standard college tech fest. It's a proving ground: hackathons that test your architecture under pressure, coding battles across languages, workshops from engineers who've shipped at scale, and a quiz arena for the truly obsessive.

One campus. Multiple realities of skill. Every participant walks out with something they didn't carry in.

Organized by the GeeksForGeeks Student Chapter at Bennett University, Greater Noida.

What awaits

Six arenas across realities

Hackathon

24 hours. Build something real. Ship or vanish. Teams of 2–4 tackle a live problem statement and present to a panel of industry judges.

Coding Battles

Head-to-head algorithmic duels on a live leaderboard. Three rounds, escalating difficulty. Only the precise survive.

Tech Quiz

From kernel trivia to framework lore — a rapid-fire quiz that rewards depth, not surface-level knowledge.

Workshops

Hands-on, instructor-led sessions covering real tools and workflows — not product demos disguised as learning.

Networking

Unstructured time with speakers, sponsors, and peers who build things. The conversations you'll remember longer than the prizes.

Prizes

₹50,000+ in prize pool across categories. Certificates for all participants. Internship fast-tracks for top performers.
